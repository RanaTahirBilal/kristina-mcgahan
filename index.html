The practice

Twenty-eight years,
four specialties,
one standard.

Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN has built a nursing career spanning more than twenty-eight years, working across cardiovascular care, nursing education and nurse wellness. She serves as a nurse consultant, a recovery coach, and a clinical nurse coordinator for a cardiac care unit.

Her clinical ground is cardiac and telemetry nursing, progressive care, medical‑surgical nursing and cardiovascular services. Alongside the bedside work she has held leadership, educator and preceptor roles inside hospital systems — building orientation programs, mentoring new nurses, and holding teams to a standard of care that does not soften at three in the morning.

Burnout Recovery

Ms. McGahan founded the Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit out of her own experience of professional burnout. It is an initiative built to help nurses process workplace stress and trauma, and to develop healthier approaches to their personal and professional wellbeing.

Through recovery coaching and educational programs she now supports nurses nationwide in rebuilding balance, confidence and career satisfaction — work she plans to keep expanding.

  • Burnout recovery coachingOne-to-one work with nurses carrying workplace stress and trauma.
  • Nursing educationTelemetry and cardiovascular teaching for bedside and orientation.
  • ConsultingClinical care, leadership and healthcare operations.
